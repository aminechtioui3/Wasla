# Wasla Brand Asset Handoff

Place your final visual assets in this folder to replace the demo placeholders used across the site.

Suggested assets to deliver:

- `wasla-logo.svg`: primary wordmark or horizontal lockup for header and footer
- `wasla-mark.svg`: compact app icon or emblem for badges and favicon refinements
- `pattern-01.svg` or `pattern-01.png`: branded mesh, geometric pattern, or subtle grain background
- `ui-shot-01.png` and `ui-shot-02.png`: high-quality mobile app screenshots with transparent or clean backgrounds
- `photo-brand-01.jpg`: Tunisian SME or creator lifestyle photography
- `photo-brand-02.jpg`: creator-at-work or product-shoot imagery
- `stickers-ui.svg`: small badges, floating chips, or branded shape accents for hero sections

Best design extras to make the site feel more human and attractive:

- A polished logo lockup in light and dark versions
- A repeating background pattern with subtle texture
- 2 to 4 authentic lifestyle photos from Tunisian creators or merchants
- 2 to 3 crisp mobile app screenshots with realistic content
- A small sticker pack: arrows, badges, hand-drawn circles, or approval stamps
- A lightweight grain or paper texture to reduce the "too perfect" SaaS feel
